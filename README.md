### Research Overview

I am a **Research Scientist at Yuansiwujie (源思无界)** and a **Google Developer Expert (GDE) in Machine Learning**. With 8+ years of industrial heterogeneous computing and safety-critical AI systems engineering experience (**Aptiv**, **Innovusion/Seyond**, **Snowlake Technology**), my research bridges probabilistic foundation models and deterministic, high-reliability execution environments across three core pillars:

1. **Clinical AI & Biomedical Reasoning Agents**: Training-free *Executor-Analyst* architectures and stratified multi-agent ensemble reasoning across 211 biomedical tools (`PubMed`, `OpenFDA`, `ChEMBL`, `ClinicalTrials.gov`). Co-author of **CureAgent** ([arXiv:2512.05576](https://arxiv.org/abs/2512.05576)), **2nd Place Winner in the CURE-Bench Competition (Track 2) at NeurIPS 2025**.
2. **Real-Time Full-Duplex Conversational Agents**: Proactive turn-taking at Transition Relevance Places ($\text{TRP} \pm 200\text{ ms}$) governed by the **Three-Factor Multiplicative Utility Model** $U(t) = A(t) \times S(t) \times N(t)$ and the **Three-Question Validity Filter** ($Q_1, Q_2, Q_3$), integrated with pure-NumPy 24/7 full-duplex runtime state machines (`qwen-voice-agent`).
3. **Embodied AI & Humanoid Robotics Sim-to-Real Harnesses**: Hardware-in-the-loop (HIL) safety verification gates (`G1_TORQUE_LIMIT` to `G5_ESTOP_LATENCY`) and **Four-Layer Blameless Postmortem Attribution** for humanoid robot locomotion transfer (`Isaac Gym` / `MuJoCo` $\rightarrow$ **Unitree G1**).
